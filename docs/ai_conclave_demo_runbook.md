## 2. Seven-Step Live Demonstration Script

### Step 1: Ambient Field Weather Context Retrieval
- **Action:** Open the AgriMind Android app. On the Home / Diagnosis screen, tap the Location & Weather banner or select **R.S. Pura, Jammu**.
- **Backend API:** `GET /weather/live?latitude=32.61&longitude=74.73`
- **What to Demonstrate to Evaluators:**
  - Real-time meteorological data retrieved directly from the **Open-Meteo REST API** (temperature ~22.8°C, humidity ~48%, condition summary, and 24h precipitation forecast).
  - Explicit attribution to Open-Meteo with live retrieval timestamps.
  - Explanation: Weather context informs disease epidemiological risk; it does not replace visual pathogen identification.

### Step 2: Rapid Image Inference & Low-Confidence Policy
- **Action:** Select a tomato leaf specimen photo from the gallery (`PlantVillage` traceable specimen `Tomato___Bacterial_spot` or take photo with device camera).
- **Canopy Context:** Input 15% leaf damage, lower canopy spread, flowering stage.
- **Backend API:** `POST /diagnose` (Multipart upload)
- **What to Demonstrate to Evaluators:**
  - **Measured Latency Reporting:** Point to the latency metrics displayed below the diagnosis card:
    - *Vision Inference Latency:* ~16.9s–17.2s (forward pass through ResNet-50 on CPU).
    - *Multi-Agent Workflow Latency:* ~41.2s–43.2s (LangGraph Investigation, ChromaDB RAG retrieval, Risk Assessment, and Critic verification).
  - **Critic Agent Verification:** Critic verification badge ("Critic Verified: True").
  - **Low-Confidence Policy Demonstration:**
    - Show that high-confidence predictions (> 65%) render immediate verified recommendations.
    - Explain the policy triggered for low-confidence (< 65%) or ambiguous specimens: an amber alert card advises the grower to retake the photo under diffused daylight at 15–20 cm distance, or submit the case for manual university extension review.

### Step 3: Context-Aware Preventive IPM Actions & University Grounding
- **Action:** Review the recommended management plan generated by the multi-agent pipeline.
- **What to Demonstrate to Evaluators:**
  - **Cultural Controls:** Immediate canopy sanitization, drip irrigation conversion to eliminate leaf wetness, and destruction of infected lower biomass.
  - **Chemical Controls:** Locally approved protectants (copper hydroxide / fixed copper bactericides) with explicit EPA/extension safety notices.
  - **University Citations:** Expand the citation list showing accredited sources (Cornell University Extension, UC IPM, Purdue Extension, UF/IFAS).

### Step 4: Evidence-Grounded Conversational Follow-up
- **Action:** Tap **"Ask AgriMind Assistant About This Case"** or navigate to the Assistant tab.
- **Query (Spoken or Typed):**
  - English: *"What is the recommended reapplication interval for copper bactericide during rain in Jammu?"*
  - Hindi: *"टमाटर के पत्तों पर तांबे के स्प्रे का छिड़काव कितने दिनों बाद करना चाहिए?"*
  - Dogri: *"टमाटर दे बूटे पर तांबे दा स्प्रे कुस चाल्ली ते कदू करना चाहिदा?"*
- **Backend API:** `POST /chat`
- **What to Demonstrate to Evaluators:**
  - Language detection routes the query accurately.
  - The assistant's response is strictly grounded in university extension literature and anchored to the active diagnostic case ID.
  - Transparent boundary: If literature is insufficient, the system provides a safe fallback without hallucinating chemical mixtures.

### Step 5: Farmer Social Network (Community Sharing, Observations & Outcome Tracking)
- **Action:** On the Case Details screen, tap **"Share Specimen with Cooperative"**.
- **Backend API:** `POST /cases/share`
- **What to Demonstrate to Evaluators:**
  - **Privacy Safeguards:** PII masking and GPS fuzzing (~11 km regional radius) to protect farmer confidentiality.
  - **Cryptographic Share Token:** Share token generated (`tok_...`).
  - **Farmer Community Observations:** Post a new field observation:
    - *"Applied copper oxychloride at 2.5 g/L; lesion expansion halted after 48 hours."*
    - Notice the clear badge distinguishing **"Farmer Observation"** (`is_ai: false`) from **"AI System Guidance"** (`is_ai: true`).
  - **Treatment Outcome Tracking:** Select **"Helped"** under Treatment Outcome Tracking and save. The case status updates to `HELPED` in the cooperative registry.

### Step 6: Cooperative-to-Cooperative Regional Disease Surveillance
- **Action:** Navigate to the History / Surveillance tab and switch to **"Cooperative Surveillance"**.
- **Backend API:** `GET /cooperative/insights` & `GET /cooperative/comparison`
- **What to Demonstrate to Evaluators:**
  - **Jammu & Kashmir Cooperative Pilot:** Aggregated disease prevalence across shared member cases, dominant pathogen identification, and collective IPM alerts.
  - **Multi-Region Comparison Grid:** Compare Jammu (R.S. Pura Hub) vs Punjab Border Grid vs Himachal Foothills.
  - **Transparent Boundary Disclosure:** Highlight the explicit disclaimer:
    - *"Multi-cooperative comparison presents local live pilot cases alongside simulated regional partner nodes for demonstration of multi-cooperative interoperability. Formal inter-state data exchange requires approved data governance protocols."*

### Step 7: Crop-Health Diagnostic PDF Report & Certification Boundary
- **Action:** Tap **"Export PDF Report"** in Case Details or **"Export Cooperative Health Audit (PDF)"** in Cooperative Surveillance.
- **Rendering:** Native Android `PdfDocument` generated instantly in device cache.
- **What to Demonstrate to Evaluators:**
  - **Multi-Page Layout:** Case metadata, specimen findings, measured inference latency, IPM actions, and university citations.
  - **Regulatory Certification Boundary Disclaimer:** Show the bolded notice at the bottom of the report:
    - *"REGULATORY NOTICE & CERTIFICATION BOUNDARY: This document is an automated AI-assisted preliminary diagnostic evaluation generated by the AgriMind decision support platform. AI image analysis alone does NOT issue an official disease-free certificate. Any formal phytosanitary certification requires an authorized inspection and approved certification process by competent state agricultural authorities."*

## 3. Fallback & Low-Connectivity Demonstration

1. **Simulated Server Disconnect (Airplane Mode):**
   - Toggle Airplane Mode on the Android device.
   - Submit a diagnosis or case share: the application displays the **Offline Queued State**, securely buffering the image and metadata in SQLite (`offline_action_queue`).
   - The farmer can still view local case history, review prior IPM recommendations, and generate PDF reports offline.
   - Disable Airplane Mode: tap **"Sync Queue"**; buffered submissions synchronize automatically to the server with duplicate prevention guards.
2. **Weather API Outage:**
   - If Open-Meteo REST API is unreachable, the system automatically falls back to cached regional seasonal baselines without crashing.
